Age of Acquisition

AOA

Led by the critical period hypothesis, Age of Acquisition (AoA) is a recurring key predictor across cognitive science and second language acquisition. Yet in previous research on word learning, AoA data have come almost entirely from questionnaires reported by parents (or even by the learners themselves!). Without real-time tracking, can anyone really remember at what age their child (or they themselves) learned a particular word?

As a researcher and as the father of a child, I have recorded her first language (Chinese) spoken vocabulary since the day she was born (counting a word only once she can recall and use it correctly without any prompt), and after we moved to Japan I began recording her second language (Japanese) spoken vocabulary alongside it. I would encourage more researchers to keep similar longitudinal records.
