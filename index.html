Salvador Xiongfei Wang

Welcome. I am a graduate student at the Graduate School of International Cultural Studies, Tohoku University, in Sendai, Japan, where I also work as a teaching assistant of Spanish and English.

I study second language acquisition — how learners build a spoken vocabulary, and what teaching and testing choices make that learning stick. My work combines classroom experiments with computational modeling and computer assisted language learning.

Before returning to academia I spent a decade in Mexico and China working in international communications and tourism. I still photograph the places my work takes me — those are on the Photos page.
